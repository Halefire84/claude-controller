# CRTC AI Receptionist: 7:00 AM Owner Presentation Package

Audience: about 15 local business owners. Target: 5-7 minutes including the 77-second video.
Goal: 3-5 owners agree to a follow-up conversation. Do not try to sell the room.

Source note: this package is built from your written description of the video. I did not view the video or hear its audio. Check the "what the video shows" list in Section 9 against the actual file before you go.

---

## 1. FINAL SPEAKING SCRIPT

**Opening (about 45 sec)**
"Good morning, everyone. I'm Chris Hale with CRTC AI, here in Mount Pleasant. We build software, websites and automation for local businesses.

I want to show you one problem almost every service business knows. A customer calls while you're on a job, or with another customer, or after you've closed for the day. And you don't pick up.

That call might have been your next job."

**Engage the room (about 60 sec)**
"Quick show of hands. Who here has missed a customer call in the last month because you were working, driving, or just couldn't get to the phone?

[Pause. Let hands go up. Then ask one person:] What happened with that call? Did they call back, or did they call someone else?

[Listen. Say:] That's exactly what I'm talking about."

**Video introduction (about 20 sec)**
"I'm going to show you a short demo. It's fictional. The business is made up, Magnolia Lawn Care, and so is the customer. It shows what we've designed our receptionist to do when the owner can't answer. It's 77 seconds."

**[PLAY VIDEO, 77 sec]**

**After the video (about 60 sec)**
"Notice what happened. The phone got answered, but that's not the main point.

It asked what the customer needed. It collected their name, number and address. In this demo it booked an estimate for Thursday at 10 AM. And the owner got a text with all of it.

So instead of an empty voicemail at 6:42 at night, the owner has a job request with the details. That's the idea we're building around: when you can't answer, the inquiry still gets captured and organized."

**Honest framing (about 30 sec)**
"I want to be straight with you. That was a demonstration of the design. Every business handles calls differently, so I'm not going to tell you one setup fits everybody."

**Close (about 40 sec)**
"If you're curious, here's what I'd like to do. Find me afterward. Tell me how calls get handled in your business today, and we'll see if this makes sense for you. No pressure. If it doesn't fit, I'll tell you that.

Thank you. I'll be right over here."

## 3. VIDEO PLAYBACK CUES

- **Before:** Video loaded, volume tested, phone on Do Not Disturb, auto-lock off, brightness up.
- **Say before pressing play:** "It's fictional. 77 seconds."
- **During:** Stay quiet. Watch the room, not the screen.
- **At the end:** Pause 2 seconds, then start "After the video."
- **Do not** replay, skip ahead, or edit the video. Use it as it is.

## 4. PLAN A / PLAN B

**Plan A: a TV or projector is available**
- Connect and test before the room fills (arrive early).
- Play the video full screen for everyone.
- Offer one-on-one replays on your phone afterward.

**Plan B: no screen**
- Skip the video during the talk. Do not pass your phone around the room.
- Replace the video block with a spoken walkthrough: "Picture a landscaping company. A customer calls at 6:42 PM, the owner is still on a job site. The receptionist answers, asks what they need, takes their name, number and address, books an estimate for Thursday morning, and texts the owner the details."
- Say: "I have a 77-second video of this. Find me afterward and I'll show you."
- Show the video one owner at a time on your phone, with sound on.

**If the audio fails:** Play it anyway only if there are captions. If not, switch to the Plan B spoken walkthrough.

## 5. OBJECTION ANSWERS

Keep each answer under 20 seconds. Never promise what is not verified.

**"How much does it cost?"**
"It depends on how your business takes calls and what you need booked. I'd want to understand your workflow first so the number I give you is accurate. Let's talk afterward."

**"Is it reliable?"**
"Fair question. Nothing is perfect, and I won't promise that. What I can do is show you how it handles your kinds of calls, and we'd test it on your workflow before you rely on it."

**"Is it replacing my staff?"**
"No. The idea is to catch the calls nobody can get to: after hours, on a job site, or when the line is busy. Your people still do the work."

**"Can it book into my schedule?"**
"In the demo it booked a Thursday estimate. How it connects to your specific calendar is something I'd need to look at with you. I don't want to guess."

**"Does it work with my phone / phone system?"**
"UNKNOWN until I know what you use. Tell me your setup and I'll give you a straight answer afterward."

**"How long does setup take?"**
"UNKNOWN in general, because it depends on your business. I'll give you a real timeline after we talk about your process."

**"Who else is using it?"**
"I'm not going to claim customers I can't back up. What you saw is a demonstration. If you're interested, we can talk about a pilot."
