# CRTC AI Client Demo Script (small businesses, possible SBA-loan context)

Length: about 20 minutes. Audience: owners of local small businesses, some of whom may be preparing an SBA loan application.

## Ground rules
- Do not give financing advice or state SBA eligibility, rates or terms. Say: "Your lender or an SBA resource partner can speak to that."
- Never present a demo page as the client's finished site. Keep the draft banner on.
- Quote only these prices: Fast Launch $499, E-Commerce $1,499, Care $99/mo, AI Receptionist $299 setup + $99/mo flat.

## 1. Open (2 min)
- "Tell me about the business. Where do you lose time or leads today?"
- Write down their answer. You will refer back to it in steps 4 and 5.

## 2. Their site today (3 min)
- Share a short audit of their current site: 3-4 specific problems (speed, mobile layout, no clear call to action, no contact form).
- Prep: run the "Audit this website" job in Claude Controller for each client beforehand.

## 3. Live demo page (5 min)
- Show a demo page for a business like theirs: draft banner, hero, service cards, contact form, CRTC footer.
- Prep: run the "Build demo page" job for their trade. Open every button and the form before the meeting and confirm each works.

## 4. Why it matters if they are seeking a loan (3 min)
- A lender or reviewer who looks you up should find a professional, current website and a phone line that gets answered.
- Keep it to that point. No promises about approval.

## 5. AI Receptionist (4 min)
- Show it answering a call or text and capturing the lead.
- Price: $299 setup + $99/mo flat.

## 6. Pricing and next step (3 min)
- Recommend ONE tier based on what they said in step 1.
- Fast Launch $499 | E-Commerce $1,499 | Care $99/mo.
- Close: "Want me to start your draft this week?" Book a date.

## Before the meeting checklist
- [ ] Audit run for each client
- [ ] Demo page built, draft banner on, all buttons and forms tested
- [ ] Pricing page open
- [ ] Laptop charged, screen share tested
- [ ] Follow-up email drafted

## After the meeting
- Send a recap with the recommended tier and the agreed next step within 24 hours.
